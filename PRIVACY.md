# Privacy

Euthus does not have accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. It does not send your sermons, transcripts or
settings to the Author or to anyone else.

**On your Mac:** speech recognition, sermon notes and slides are produced on the Mac with Apple's on-device
frameworks. Transcripts, notes, songs and settings are stored in `~/Library/Application Support/Euthus` and a log in
`~/Library/Logs/Euthus.log`. You can delete them at any time.

**When Euthus uses the network:**
- to download Apple's speech recognition model (handled by macOS);
- to check https://github.com/Rtnkr/euthus for updates (Sparkle; GitHub sees an ordinary download request);
- on your local network only, for features you turn on: NDI outputs, linking two Macs, the phone remote and OSC.

**Microphone:** used only while Listen or the microphone test is running.

If you send feedback or attach a log to an issue, that is public on GitHub. Read the log first and remove anything
you would rather not share.
